# Introduction

Whether a bladder tumour invades the detrusor muscle decides between local treatment and radical cystectomy or multimodal bladder preservation [@primer; @eau_mibc], yet transurethral resection under-stages a substantial fraction of tumours [@understaging]. MRI read with the Vesical Imaging-Reporting and Data System (VI-RADS) detects muscle invasion accurately, and an MRI-first pathway shortened the time to correct treatment in the BladderPath randomised trial [@vi_rads; @virads_meta; @bladderpath_jco; @bladderpath_hta]. Because reliable VI-RADS reading requires experience, automated MRI assessment has been studied widely.

Radiomics and deep-learning models report areas under the ROC curve (AUCs) of 0.82–0.97 for MRI-based prediction of muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC) versus non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC), and a meta-analysis pooled an AUC of 0.88 [@zheng_fo; @li_cmpb; @kurata; @rad_meta]. Most models were developed and validated at one centre; where external data were available, performance fell, for example from 0.96 to 0.86 [@li_er]. On the public four-centre FedBCa dataset [@fedbca], published AUCs range from 0.82–0.87 [@fedbca; @jiim_fed] to 0.99 [@dadcnet], a spread too wide to reflect model capacity alone.

Evaluation leakage is a recognised cause of over-optimism in medical machine learning [@kapoor; @varoquaux; @rosenblatt]. Typical forms are adjacent slices of one tumour on both sides of a split [@yagis; @tampu], several tumours of one patient treated as independent, features selected on the full dataset [@demircioglu; @gidwani], and models or training epochs chosen by test performance. Multicentre data add a further risk, because a model can recognise the scanner or centre instead of the disease [@zech; @degrave].

How much each of these flaws inflates MRI-based prediction of muscle invasion has not been measured. On FedBCa, we therefore benchmarked pre-specified radiomics, deep-learning and fusion models against tumour size under patient-grouped and leave-one-centre-out validation. We then measured how much each common evaluation flaw inflates the AUC of the same models on the same data, and how strongly image features encode the acquiring centre.
